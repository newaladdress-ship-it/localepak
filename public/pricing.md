# Pricing — LocalePak

Listing prices for LocalePak (https://www.localepak.com). All prices are in Pakistani
Rupees (PKR) and are **one-time per listing** — there is no monthly
subscription.

## Listing tiers

| Tier | Price (PKR) | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | 99 | Listed on LocalePak after approval. Shows in search, city and category pages. No edits after submission. |
| **Premium** | 199 | Everything in Basic, plus: edit your listing (via approval), and up to 3 projects on your listing. |
| **Featured** | 299 | Everything in Premium, plus: up to 5 projects on your listing, and publish up to 2 blog posts. |

Notes:

- A Basic listing can never be edited. The owner can delete the
  listing and submit a new one instead.
- Premium and Featured owners request edits from their dashboard; an
  approved edit goes live after review.
- Projects live on the listing itself. Blog posts (Featured only) are
  reviewed by an admin before publication.
- The same three tiers apply to business listings, company profiles,
  job posts and professional profiles.

## How to pay

Pay the exact tier amount by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer
(Mashreq Bank), then upload your payment screenshot on the website
during submission. Your listing is published after admin approval of
both the listing and the payment proof.

## What is free

- Browsing the directory, reading listings, and reading blog guides:
  free, no account needed.
- Creating a website account: free.
- The public read-only API (`/api/v1/*`) and the MCP server (`/api/mcp`):
  free, no key, no signup. Reads are limited to 60 requests per minute
  per IP, with at most 20 results per page. There is no paid API tier.
- Listing fees above apply only when you submit a listing.
